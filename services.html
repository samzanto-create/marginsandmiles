Frequently Asked Questions

Common Questions. Clear Answers.

How long does a strategy call take?

About 20 minutes. We'll ask a few questions about your spending and travel goals, then walk you through where the opportunity is.

Can I use my existing credit cards?

In most cases, yes. We'll build your strategy around the cards you already have, then recommend additions only where they clearly add value.

Do I need a business to work with you?

Yes — our strategies are built around business spending, typically $2,000–$5,000+ per month across cards.

Do you book travel for me?

We guide you through booking the best redemptions yourself, and offer hands-on support for more complex bookings.

Will I have to spend more money?

No. The goal is to earn more from the spending you're already doing — not to encourage spending you wouldn't otherwise make.

Will this hurt my credit score?

We factor your credit profile into every recommendation and talk through any impact honestly before you apply for anything.
