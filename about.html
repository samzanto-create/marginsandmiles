My Story

Numbers, Systems, and a Genuine Love of Travel

I've always been the kind of person who likes things to add up. Long before Margins and Miles existed, I spent my career in the world of numbers, systems, and small business finances — helping business owners get a clear, honest picture of where their money actually goes. That background is where this business was born.

Somewhere along the way, I noticed something that most business owners never stop to think about: the money already moving through their business every month was quietly capable of paying for incredible travel — first class flights, luxury hotels, entire vacations — if it was routed the right way. Most people were leaving that value on the table simply because nobody had ever shown them how to capture it.

So I started applying the same discipline I bring to numbers and systems to credit card rewards, points programs, and redemption strategy. I tested it relentlessly on my own spending long before I ever offered it to a client. What started as a personal project turned into a genuine obsession — and eventually, into Margins and Miles.

Today, I help business owners do exactly what I did for myself: turn ordinary, everyday spending into extraordinary travel, without changing how they run their business and without sacrificing profitability or sound expense management. I still get a genuine thrill every time a client books a trip they never thought their business could pay for.
